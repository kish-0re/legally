About Us

At Legally, we believe legal documents shouldn’t feel like a trap of jargon and fine print. Everyone deserves to understand what they’re signing — whether it’s a rental agreement, a loan contract, or terms of service.

We built Legally as your legal buddy — a friendly assistant that takes confusing contracts and turns them into plain, simple English.

Using the power of AI + Google Cloud, Legally:

  • Reads your documents (PDFs, images, scans).
  • Summarizes them in clear language.
  • Flags potential red flags or tricky clauses.
  • Lets you ask follow-up questions, just like you’d ask a friend who knows law.

Our mission is to empower everyday people — students, tenants, freelancers, small business owners — to make informed decisions without needing a lawyer by their side for every contract.

Legally isn’t a replacement for professional legal advice, but it’s your first line of clarity — making law more accessible, less intimidating, and more human.

🌟 Our Vision

A world where no one signs blindly. Legal knowledge should be accessible, understandable, and empowering for everyone.
